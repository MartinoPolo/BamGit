design notes
Sparkline as data enrichment

The inline SVG sparkline adds visual context that raw numbers cannot convey: direction, volatility, and recency of spend. A 7-point polyline (one per day) shows the trailing week at a glance. The terminal dot marks "now" as an anchor point.

Progressive disclosure

At rest: cost value + muted sparkline. The trend is visible but doesn't demand attention. On hover: the entire element shifts to --primary, the sparkline brightens, and a structured tooltip shows the precise delta and direction label. Three layers of detail without leaving the inline context.

Inline without disruption

The sparkline is sized relative to the text: 28x10px at md, 24x8px at sm. It sits on the same baseline as the cost text, using inline-flex alignment. This avoids vertical layout shifts in stat rows, card footers, and prose contexts. The subtle background tint on hover creates a hit target without adding visual weight at rest.
