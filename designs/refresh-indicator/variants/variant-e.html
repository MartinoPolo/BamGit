RefreshIndicator
Variant E — Animated Countdown
A tiny countdown timer (2m 15s) sits next to the refresh icon button, showing exactly when the next auto-refresh will fire. The countdown text is subtle when data is fresh and becomes progressively more prominent as data ages. A circular progress ring on the icon button depletes as the countdown ticks. When the countdown reaches zero, auto-refresh triggers. Manual refresh resets the countdown. A dot badge appears when new data arrives mid-countdown.
