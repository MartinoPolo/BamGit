Anatomy

Icon button — 26 × 26px Secondary ghost variant. Transparent bg at rest, surface-2 on hover. No label — tooltip carries all context.
Tooltip — above, centered Appears on hover. Dark pill on dark bg for maximum contrast. Optional second line carries mono timestamp or sub-label.
Dot badge — 7px, top-right Shown only in new-data state. Green fill, 1.5px border separating it from button corner. Pulses twice then settles.
Border pulse — new-data state Subtle breathing animation on the border, looping indefinitely. Paired with a faint green box-shadow at 50% of cycle.

Behavior notes

  • Idle — transparent bg, foreground-subtle icon. Tooltip reads "Refresh metrics". Full hover/active states apply.
  • Loading — icon spins at 900 ms/rev. Button is disabled, pointer-events none. Tooltip reads "Refreshing…".
  • Fresh — icon tinted status-success, border adopts faint green. A one-shot background flash fires on mount. Settles to low-opacity green bg.
  • Stale — whole button fades to 60% opacity. Icon color reverts to foreground-subtle. Tooltip shows elapsed time + mono timestamp on a second line.
  • New data — dot badge appears top-right, pulses twice (scale 1 → 1.55) then settles. Border breathes with a 2s pulse loop. Tooltip urgent: "New data available / click to refresh".
  • Tooltip — never shown simultaneously with another tooltip. Delay 400 ms on enter, 100 ms on leave. Positioned above center; flips to below if insufficient viewport space.
